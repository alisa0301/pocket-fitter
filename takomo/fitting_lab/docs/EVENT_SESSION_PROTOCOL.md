# Simulator visit: Saturday 7 November, 11.00–12.00, Takomo Golf

Takomo has booked its simulator (TrackMan) for the teams on **Saturday 7 November, 11.00–12.00**. If many people come, the group is split in two and each half gets 30 minutes. Exact meeting point and group split: Discord on Saturday morning.

## What happens

Takomo's own players hit shots in different ways while explaining what changes, and teams film freely. You can see real launch-monitor numbers and record video of known swings, which is exactly what this pack's synthetic data cannot give you.

## Make the most of 30 minutes

1. **Prepare before you go.** Know which numbers your idea needs (speed? strike location? lie at impact?). Bring a phone that films 240 fps slow motion, a tripod or a steady friend, and the printed measurement sheet.
2. **Film two angles.** Face-on (camera level with the hands, square to the target line) and down-the-line (behind the hands, along the target line). Keep the whole club in frame and do not zoom.
3. **Write down every shot.** Use `event_kit/shot_log.csv`: who hit, which club, what the hitter was trying to do (normal, toe strike, heel strike, flat or upright lie, different shaft) and what TrackMan showed. Photograph the TrackMan screen for each shot if there is no export. A video without its numbers is worth much less.
4. **If there is time, ask** whether a ball-line test can be shown: a marker line on the ball, a few shots, a photo of the face. It is the quickest way to see what lie evidence looks like on a real club. Takomo has not promised it; it depends on the session.
5. **Ask questions.** What does a fitter look at first? Which answers in today's questionnaire do they distrust most? Quotes from a fitter are strong material for your pitch.
